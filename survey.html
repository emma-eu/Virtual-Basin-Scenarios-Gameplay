Help shape the future of water in the basin

How would you design a plan for the Great Salt Lake Basin’s water supply?

Build a package of eight water strategies, decide how their water benefits should be shared between Basin Supply and the Great Salt Lake, and see how your choices perform under an uncertain water future.

This is a feedback activity, not a test. There is no single correct package. We want to understand which strategies you would choose, which trade-offs you are willing to make, and what you want decision-makers to know.

Responses are anonymous. The survey does not ask for your name or email. ZIP code and basin connection are optional.

⚠
BEFORE STEP 2 · WATER ALLOCATION

Choose your water allocation wisely

In the next step, you'll draw a possible future climate. Dry conditions could remove several water points from both Great Salt Lake and Basin Supply.

Keep in mind: Basin Supply must have at least 4 points after the water future to meet basin needs. Extra Supply points provide a buffer, while Lake points can improve ecosystem health and reduce dust.
