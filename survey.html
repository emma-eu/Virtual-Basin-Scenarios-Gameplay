Shape the future of water in the basin

What trade-offs would you make?

Build a package of eight water strategies, decide how their water benefits should be shared between basin supply and the Great Salt Lake, and see how your choices perform under an uncertain water future.

This is a feedback activity, not a test. There is no single correct package. We are interested in which trade-offs you make and what you want decision-makers to understand.

Responses are anonymous. The survey does not ask for your name or email. An optional ZIP code and basin connection question help us understand who participated.
